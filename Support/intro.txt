About the Project i work on NSE :

We have multiple application backend & frontend application, where backend application are trading related where we disseminate Real time trading data in various levels. (level 1, level 2, level 3 and tick by tick) across segment's Capital Market (CM), Futures & Options (F&O), Currency Derivative (CD) and also Tick By Tick data feed of NSE. It disseminates information about orders and trades on a real time basis.
Typically this data is disimminated through exchange where over the multicast ip and port we connect to it and further we disiminate to our client through our Points of Presence (POPs). Members connect to these POPs over dedicated leased lines, typically managed by SIFY.
Also we have frontedn external and internal application which we monitor and provide support in case of any issue here web server used is Apchae Tomcat & Database is Oracle DB.


My responsibilities included monitoring the multicast feed infrastructure, troubleshooting data dissemination issues, coordinating with network teams and SIFY for connectivity problems, and ensuring uninterrupted delivery of market data to members.
