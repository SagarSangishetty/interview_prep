Scenario 2: CI Packet Missing in CM Realtime Index Feed

Issue:
- NIFTY 50 CI packet was not disseminated to external clients through the CM Realtime Index Feed application.

Investigation:
- Verified the issue from application logs.
- Coordinated with the development team.
- Compared source packet with outgoing packet.

Root Cause:
- During application initialization, the first packet was getting dropped.
- The first packet contained the NIFTY 50 CI packet.
- As a result, external clients did not receive the NIFTY 50 realtime index data.

Immediate Fix:
- Re-disseminated the missing CI packets to restore the service.

Permanent Fix:
- Development team modified the FCM_ID.DAT processing by adding a dummy packet at the beginning.
- The initialization process discarded the dummy packet instead of the actual NIFTY 50 packet.
- After deployment, monitored the feed and verified successful packet dissemination.

Learning:
- Always differentiate between immediate workaround and permanent fix.
- Validate packet flow after deployment before closing the incident.
